# Planday

> Planday (part of Xero) is a workforce management platform for shift-based businesses in hospitality, retail, healthcare, hotels, entertainment, and fitness. Its public REST API, secured by OAuth2 bearer tokens, is organized into domains — HR, Absence, Pay, Payroll, Portal, Punchclock, Reports, Revenue, Schedule, Contract Rules, and Security Group Membership — for syncing employees, shifts, timesheets, pay rates, and cost data with external systems.

## APIs

- [Planday API](https://openapi.planday.com/): REST/JSON API. Base host https://openapi.planday.com, paths of the form /{domain}/v{version}/... Auth via OAuth2 bearer token plus X-ClientId header. Most list endpoints paginate at 50 records/page.

## Authentication

- [Authorization flow](https://openapi.planday.com/gettingstarted/authorization-flow/): OAuth2. Token endpoint https://id.planday.com/connect/token, authorize endpoint https://id.planday.com/connect/authorize. Access tokens expire after 1 hour; refresh tokens are non-expiring.
- [OpenID discovery](https://id.planday.com/.well-known/openid-configuration): full OIDC metadata and the complete scopes_supported list (resource:action scopes such as employee:read, shift:create, absence:update).
